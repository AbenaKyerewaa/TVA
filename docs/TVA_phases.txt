
LUXURY JEWELRY E-COMMERCE PLATFORM
Complete Development Roadmap — 20 Phases
Recommended Stack: PHP, Laravel, MySQL, Blade, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript, Vite.
Project Objective
To develop a premium, modern, fully responsive jewelry e-commerce platform that provides customers with an elegant shopping experience while giving administrators complete control over products, orders, customers, inventory, payments, and business operations.
⸻
